## The study in brief
- **Question/gap:** rectum→vagina same-strain transfer is documented for the pathogens by culture, but never
  resolved *in situ, longitudinally, at strain level* in a low-resource pregnancy cohort.
- **Aims:** (1) does it happen · (2) direction & which organisms · (3) does it cause disease · (4) who's at
  risk (WASH/access).
- **Design:** ~350 pregnant women across a WASH gradient; paired rectal+vaginal deep shotgun at 3–4
  timepoints; **strainshare** as the analysis engine (validated on 26 women of public data).
- **Feasibility/power:** the WASH-association arm is binding (~180 evaluable for a 20-pt difference);
  ~350 enrolled → ~180–220 evaluable. Order-of-magnitude budget: ~$2.5–3.7M direct (R01-scale), or a
  ~$0.4–0.7M R21 pilot.
- **Timeliness:** the hexavalent maternal GBS vaccine (GBS6) is in Phase 3 — our clone-level typing
  (CC17/serotype III, ST131) informs vaccine serotype coverage.
