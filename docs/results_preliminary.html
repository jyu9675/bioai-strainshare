Preliminary Strain-Sharing Results

BIOAI pilot · PRJNA288562 (Goltsman cohort) · 4 subjects, 88/88 profiles, shallow depth · Aug 2026
Bottom line. The pipeline correctly detects identical strains (positive controls pass across all 4 subjects), and across all 4 subjects with paired vaginal+gut data there is no detectable gut↔vagina strain sharing — vaginal Lactobacillus appears in the gut only as sparse, divergent traces. Still "no evidence of sharing," not "evidence of none" — depth is limiting.

Interpretation & caveats

  • Now 4 subjects, not 1: the cross-site result held in every subject with paired vaginal+gut data — a much firmer preliminary "no sharing" than the earlier single-subject look.
  • Reproduces the field: like Goltsman 2018, vaginal taxa appear in the gut but not as an identical strain — consistent with "no confirmed identical gut–vagina Lactobacillus strain" (Takada).
  • Shallow depth keeps cross-site breadth tiny — exactly the regime that would hide low-abundance sharing. This limits sensitivity, not the validity of the positive controls.
Method note. On this constrained box (15 GB RAM, near-full C:, USB data drive), inStrain compare at full scale hangs/OOMs; results came from a pairwise workaround that stages 2 profiles per comparison into RAM (scripts/05b_pairwise_compare.py). All 88/88 profiles completed with 0 failures.
