# Letters of support — targets & template

*Partners to secure for the [rectovaginal-transmission study](study-rectovaginal-pregnancy.md). Strong
letters de-risk the two things reviewers doubt most: **site access/enrollment** in low-resource settings
and **access to comparator data**. Confirm each partner's actual willingness before naming them — this is
a target list and a template, not a claim of commitment.*

## Target partners

| Partner (type) | Why | The letter should attest |
|---|---|---|
| **East African pregnancy cohort** — Kisumu (Kenya) / Kigali (Rwanda) vaginal-microbiome & preterm-birth groups | Established antenatal cohorts, high preterm rates, prior microbiome sampling | Site access, enrollment feasibility, local IRB, sample collection, in-country data terms |
| **South African pregnancy-microbiome cohort** (gut/vaginal/oral; genital-infection & gestational-age studies) | Existing paired multi-site sampling in the target population | Recruitment, sample sharing/collection, co-investigation |
| **GBS genomics groups** — Ethiopia / Nigeria / Sri Lanka isolate-WGS teams | Provide GBS strain references & regional context | Reference strain sharing, methods exchange |
| **MOMS-PI / VCU Vaginal Microbiome Consortium (GAPPS)** | Controlled comparator cohort (see [dbGaP scoping](momspi-dbgap-scoping.md)) | Data-access facilitation, comparator analysis, co-authorship terms |
| **Sequencing core** (institutional or regional) | Deep shotgun + enrichment at scale | Capacity, turnaround, pricing for ~2,450+ libraries |
| **Clinical / OB partner** | Clinician-led management of any infection found | Standard-of-care management, outcome ascertainment |
| **Bioinformatics / strain-genomics collaborator** | strainshare + MAG reference expertise | Analytic capacity, method rigor |

## What makes a letter strong

Specific commitments, not endorsements: named site(s), realistic enrollment numbers and timeline, IRB
pathway, who collects/ships samples, data-sharing and authorship terms, and any in-country data-sovereignty
arrangements. A letter that only says "this is important work" carries little weight.

## Draft letter-of-support (template — partner adapts on letterhead)

> [Date]
>
> Dear [Review Committee],
>
> I write to confirm the participation of [institution / cohort] in the study **"Rectum-to-vagina
> bacterial transmission in pregnancy,"** led by [PI]. Our group maintains [describe cohort/site: e.g., an
> antenatal cohort of ~[N] pregnant women at [location], with established microbiome sampling and local
> ethics approval].
>
> For this project we commit to: [enroll ~[N] participants over [period] / provide paired rectal and
> vaginal specimens at [n] timepoints / facilitate local IRB and data-transfer agreements / share [GBS
> reference strains / comparator data] / provide clinician-led management of any infection identified].
> [Name] will serve as [role] and dedicate [effort].
>
> We have the [clinical infrastructure / sample-collection capacity / regulatory pathway] to deliver
> these commitments and are enthusiastic about the study's potential to improve maternal and neonatal
> outcomes in [setting].
>
> Sincerely,
> [Name, title, institution]

## Sequence of asks

1. Informal scoping conversation → gauge willingness and realistic numbers.
2. Share the [Specific Aims](specific-aims.md) + [designed brief](rectovaginal-route.html).
3. Agree roles, effort, IRB pathway, data/authorship terms.
4. Request the letter with the specific commitments above (well before the deadline).
